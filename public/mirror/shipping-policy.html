    Shipping policy

    Last updated 06/11/2024

    Thank you for choosing JP for your eyewear needs. We are committed to providing you with the best shopping experience, including our shipping services. Please read our shipping policy carefully to understand our shipping procedures and options.

    1. Shipping Methods

    We offer the following shipping methods for your convenience:

    • Free Shipping (3-5 business days): Enjoy free standard shipping on all orders. Your order will typically arrive within 3 to 5 business days.
    • Next Day Shipping ($100): If you need your order in a hurry, we offer expedited next-day shipping for a fee of $100. Please note that this option is available for a faster delivery service. Please also note that not every item can be shipped on the same day. We will definitely notify you about this.

    2. Shipping Origin

    All orders are shipped from our location at:

    JP CORP. 200 W 125th Street, New York, NY 10027, United States

    3. Shipping Carrier

    We utilize the services of the United States Postal Service (FedEx) to ensure safe and reliable delivery of your eyewear products. You can trust in FedEx to handle your order with care.

    4. Contact Information

    If you have any questions, concerns, or need assistance with your order, please don't hesitate to reach out to our customer support team. You can contact us via the following methods:

    • Email: customer-services@jp.com
    • Phone:+1 347-544-5139

    Our dedicated team is here to assist you with any shipping-related inquiries and to make your shopping experience as smooth as possible.

    5. Order Tracking

    Once your order is shipped, you will receive a tracking number, which will allow you to monitor the progress of your delivery.

    6. International Shipping

    Currently, we only offer shipping services within the United States.

    Please feel free to reach out to our customer support team for any additional information or assistance regarding our shipping policy. We value your satisfaction and look forward to providing you with exceptional eyewear products and services.

    Packaging Guidelines

    Do's

    • ✓

      Use a sturdy box that's at least twice the size of your glasses case

    • ✓

      Wrap glasses in microfiber cloth and place in their original case

    • ✓

      Use bubble wrap or packing paper to fill empty spaces

    • ✓

      Include your order number and contact information

    • ✓

      Consider using a shipping service with tracking and insurance

    • ✓

      Take photos of your glasses before shipping for your records

    Don'ts

    • ✗

      Ship glasses without a protective case

    • ✗

      Use an envelope or padded mailer instead of a box

    • ✗

      Forget to secure the glasses case inside the box

    • ✗

      Ship multiple pairs loose together where they can damage each other

    • ✗

      Omit your contact information and order details

    • ✗

      Use excessive tape on the glasses themselves

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How should I package my glasses for shipping?

    Place your glasses in their original case, wrapped in a microfiber cloth. Then place the case in a sturdy box with packing material to prevent movement during transit. Ensure there's cushioning on all sides.

    Do I need to include anything with my shipment?

    Yes, please include a note with your full name, contact information, order number (if applicable), and a brief description of the service you're requesting.

    How long will it take to receive my glasses back?

    Processing time varies depending on the service requested, but approximately ranges from 7-14 business days plus shipping time. You'll receive an email with a more specific timeline once we receive your glasses.

    Is my shipment insured?

    We recommend adding shipping insurance through your chosen carrier. Once we receive your glasses, they are covered by our in-house insurance while in our possession.

    Can I track my return shipment?

    Yes, we provide tracking information via email when your glasses are shipped back to you.
